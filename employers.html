Quick answers.

Is posting really free?

Yes, during the beta. We ask for a pay range on every listing.

Are the candidates real?

Not yet. The demo uses made-up people so you can see how it works. You can also add a real candidate by pasting the CV ID link they send you.

How does ranking work?

Each applicant is scored against the job using the skills and experience on their CV, and the reasons are shown. We do not use hidden signals. You can sort another way.

Do you integrate with my applicant tracking system?

Not yet. A simple built-in board comes first.
