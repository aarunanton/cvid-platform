Quick answers.

Is it free?

Yes. Building, checking and applying are free for candidates.

Do I need an account?

Not to build or check a CV. Applying to jobs on the platform will need an account once the live version launches. Today the app is a demo that runs in your browser.

Who sees my CV?

Only people you choose. You can keep your profile private or let companies find you.

What does Verified mean?

That someone other than you confirmed it, such as an employer, a college or a professional register. The badge shows who and when.
